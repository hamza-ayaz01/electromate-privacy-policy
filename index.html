🛡 ElectroMate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

About ElectroMate

ElectroMate is an offline electronics engineering toolbox designed for students, educators, hobbyists, and professional engineers. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains how the application handles your information.

Information We Collect

ElectroMate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The application does not require:

  • User accounts
  • Login or registration
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Personal identification

Local Data Storage

To improve your experience, ElectroMate stores certain information locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

  • Bookmarks
  • Recently Used Tools
  • Theme Preference
  • Application Settings

Feedback

When you select Send Feedback, ElectroMate opens your device's default email application with a pre-filled email template.

No information is transmitted unless you choose to send the email yourself.

Internet Access

Most features of ElectroMate work completely offline.

Future versions may include optional online resources such as component libraries, datasheets, or documentation. This Privacy Policy will be updated if those features change how data is handled.

Third-Party Services

ElectroMate currently does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • User tracking
  • Cloud databases

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as new features are added. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated revision date.
